Waypoint

Plan it, play it, remember it.

Waypoint keeps every trip in one place: day-by-day plans, packing and budget, photo challenges at must-see spots around the world, and a passport of everywhere you've been.

Testing Waypoint on Android

  1. Send the developer the email address of your Google account (the one on your Android phone).
  2. Once you're added, open the test link you're sent and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install Waypoint from the Play Store link on that page.
  4. Open it, create an account, and use it for at least 14 days. Keep it installed.
  5. Send ideas and bugs from Passport > Send feedback.
