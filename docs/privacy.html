Waypoint

Privacy policy

Last updated September 30, 2026

Waypoint is a travel app made by Oakley White ("we"). This page explains what the app collects, why, and how you control it. Contact: SUPPORT_EMAIL.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, password (stored hashed by our database provider), and the display name you choose.
  • Content you add: trips, itineraries, packing lists, budgets and expenses, booking notes, bucket lists, challenge progress, badges, and the photos and notes you capture.
  • Location: only when you tap "Use my location", to show challenges near you. It is used on your device and is not stored on our servers.
  • Feedback and reports: messages you send from the app, and reports you file about other people's photos.

How it's used

To run the app: sync your data across your devices, show shared trips to the friends you invite, rank the leaderboard, and review reports. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use tracking for advertising.

Who can see what

  • Your trips, notes, photos and lists are private to your account.
  • If you share a trip, the people who join with your code see your name and the photos and notes you add to that trip.
  • Your display name, rank, points and counts (challenges, parks, places, trips, badges) appear on the in-app leaderboard to other Waypoint users.

Service providers

  • Supabase stores accounts, data and photos.
  • Anthropic (Claude) processes the text of AI requests when you use "Plan with AI", "Suggest items" or "Make challenges". Those requests include trip details such as destination, dates and planned stops. Photos are never sent.
  • Google Fonts serves the app's fonts.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while your account exists. Delete your account any time in the app under Passport > Account > Delete my account. That permanently removes your account, trips, photos, challenge progress and leaderboard entry. You can also request deletion at this page. Reports you filed are kept without your identity so we can finish reviewing them.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and database rules limit each account to its own data and the trips it has joined.

Children

Waypoint isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and note it in the app.
