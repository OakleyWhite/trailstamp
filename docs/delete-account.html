Delete your Trailstamp account

In the app (instant)

  1. Open Trailstamp and tap Passport.
  2. Scroll to Account and tap Delete my account.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm.

Without the app

Email oneforoakley@icloud.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my Trailstamp account". We'll delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

Your account, profile name, trips, plans, packing lists, budgets, notes, photos, challenge progress, badges, and leaderboard entry. Photos you added to a friend's shared trip are removed too. Reports you filed are kept without your identity so they can be reviewed.
